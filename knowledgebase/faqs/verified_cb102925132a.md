## Q: I am having trouble connecting to Campus-Student-Secure wifi from my laptop and cannot access the internal library VPN portal.

To connect to Campus-Student-Secure:
1. Select SSID 'Campus-Student-Secure' and choose EAP Method: PEAP with MSCHAPv2.
2. Use your student ID as username and your portal password.
3. For library VPN, visit https://vpn.university.edu and authenticate.
